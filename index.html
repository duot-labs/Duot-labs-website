SYSTEMS RESEARCH & INFRASTRUCTURE

Speed and throughput where it matters.

We study latency and throughput optimization in modern computing infrastructure. We bridge the gap between academic computer science papers and production engineering.

RESEARCH 01

Latency & Throughput Optimization

We profile and redesign bottlenecks in distributed pipelines, model inference, and real-time event streaming systems to reduce P99 latency and maximize compute throughput.

  • ✓ Kernel and memory cache locality optimizations
  • ✓ High-frequency event ingestion and streaming architectures
  • ✓ Distributed system profiling and tail-latency reduction

RESEARCH 02

Applying Research Papers to Production

Academic literature moves fast, but most teams struggle to implement new papers. We take state-of-the-art algorithms in ML, search, and retrieval systems and turn them into production software.

  • ✓ Semantic search, vector indexing, and embeddings retrieval
  • ✓ Fast approximate algorithms for real-time ranking and filtering
  • ✓ Converting theoretical architectures into clean, testable code

TECHNICAL AD CONSULTING

Solving hard technical problems in advertising.

Modern ad platforms operate as massive real-time auction and ML ranking systems. We help growth and ads engineering teams fix broken tracking, speed up creative testing, and scale profitably.

AD TECH 01

Server-Side Tracking & Attribution (CAPI)

Client-side pixels lose up to 30% of customer purchases due to browser ad blockers and iOS privacy restrictions. We build robust server-to-server tracking pipelines that restore clean data to your ad platforms.

  • ✓ Meta Conversions API (CAPI) & Google Offline Events
  • ✓ First-party event deduplication and zero signal loss
  • ✓ Accurate CAC, LTV, and multi-touch attribution visibility

Creative Testing Systems & Automation

Creative fatigue causes ad costs to spike. We build automated testing workflows that allow teams to test dozens of hooks, scripts, and angles weekly, finding top-performing ads before scaling budget.

  • ✓ Custom Slack bots for real-time performance alerts
